# Assets

## Instrument Serif

Instrument Serif, regular and italic Latin subsets, by the Instrument Serif Project Authors. Obtained from the `@fontsource/instrument-serif` package. Distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the full notice is in `src/assets/fonts/INSTRUMENT-SERIF-OFL.txt`.

The font files are served from this website. Visitors do not request fonts from a third-party service.

## Project artwork
